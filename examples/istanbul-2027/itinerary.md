## Halal and prayer essentials

Prayer times use the Turkish (Diyanet) method with standard Asr; they are planning estimates, so check the mosque timetable on arrival.

- **Qibla from Sultanahmet:** 152° from true north (SSE), 2,405 km to Makkah. Use the hotel marker or a qibla app in the room.
- **Jummah:** Süleymaniye Mosque on Friday 16 April. In Turkey the khutbah follows Dhuhr time (about 13:04), so arrive by 12:30.
- **Mosque visits:** tourist entry pauses around each prayer. Women cover hair; everyone covers knees and shoulders; shoes off at the door.
- **Food:** meat in local Turkish restaurants is halal by default, but many serve alcohol. Hotels in international chains may serve pork. Each food suggestion below carries an evidence label.
- **Concessions:** if you shorten or combine prayers while travelling, follow your own school; times below show all five prayers.
